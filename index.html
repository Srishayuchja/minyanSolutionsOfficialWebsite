Building Digital Excellence Since Day One

Miniyan Solutions (Pvt) Ltd is a Sri Lanka-based technology company dedicated to crafting high-quality software products and digital services. From startups to enterprises, we partner with clients to transform their vision into powerful digital experiences.

From custom web applications and mobile apps for iOS & Android, to POS systems, ride-sharing platforms, matrimony portals, and exam practice tools — we have built across diverse industries including healthcare, hospitality, education, logistics, and retail. Every project we take on is crafted with care, precision, and a commitment to long-term value.

Our team of passionate engineers, designers, and strategists work collaboratively to deliver solutions that are not just functional — but exceptional.
